About Me

Joshua De Leon

I started my GIS journey in 2024, teaching myself QGIS while studying Agricultural Engineering and working in research. Since then, I've moved from making maps to building the systems behind them, with a growing focus on programming, spatial data, databases, automation, and WebGIS.

Today, I work across GIS and software engineering, developing tools and workflows for land, utilities, and infrastructure. My work combines GIS, programming, spatial databases, CAD-to-GIS, data processing, and web technologies to turn fragmented geographic data into structured, searchable, and useful systems.
