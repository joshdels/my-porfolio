About Me

Joshua

I started my GIS journey in 2024, teaching myself QGIS while studying Agricultural Engineering and working in research. My early work and interests included hydrology, land and water resources, and spatial analysis, which gave me a practical foundation in using GIS to understand real-world geographic problems. What began as an interest in creating maps and analyzing spatial data gradually led me deeper into the technology behind GIS, spatial databases, automation, programming, cloud infrastructure, and web mapping. Today, I work at the intersection of GIS and software engineering, building systems that turn fragmented spatial data into practical tools for organizations. I’m the founder of TopMap Solutions, where I focus on spatial data modernization and GIS systems for land, cadastral, and infrastructure applications. My work involves transforming CAD drawings, paper records, spreadsheets, and legacy GIS data into structured spatial information that can be managed through modern GIS and WebGIS platforms. My experience has particularly grown around land and parcel data, including CAD-to-GIS workflows, cadastral data, spatial databases, GIS automation, and WebGIS. I’m now expanding that foundation into utility and infrastructure GIS, with a growing focus on electric distribution networks, asset mapping, and the systems that support infrastructure operations. My work combines CAD, GIS, spatial databases, Python, Javascript, C#, .Net, web technologies, and GIS automation to solve problems that are often much bigger than simply making a map. I’m particularly interested in spatial data infrastructure, GIS automation, GeoAI, cloud infrastructure, utility GIS, and intelligent mapping systems. My long-term goal is to help organizations move beyond static maps and fragmented spatial data toward spatial systems that fit naturally into their everyday workflows, connecting geographic data, operational processes, and software into tools that are actually useful in the field.
